Branch Financial Head — Operational Guide

1. Daily Collections Verification

Navigate to Approvals → Income to inspect payment proofs uploaded by counselors. Approve to record payments or deny with comments to request a re-upload.

2. Pending Payment Escalations

Under Pending Payments, review students grouped by batch start dates. Flag overdue students to counselors for follow-up before suspension triggers.

3. Monthly Payroll Runs

On the 25th of each month, navigate to HR & Payroll → Payroll. Click Generate, select payable employees, and confirm net amounts. Deferring an employee saves their balance to Salary Arrears.

4. Expenses & GST Ledger

Log all vendor payments under Expenses & Ledger. Ensure GST invoices are filed accurately to maintain compliance.
